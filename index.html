FAQ

Do I need followers or an existing audience?
No. UGC is content made for brands to use — on their pages and in their ads. Brands hire based on your portfolio, not your follower count. Most students start from zero.
How much time does it take?
Plan for 5–10 focused hours a week. The program is built to run alongside a job or studies — but it does not work on zero hours. That's why we ask about your availability in the application.
What equipment do I need?
A smartphone and decent natural light. We cover simple upgrades later, but nobody needs a camera kit to land their first deals.
How fast will I make money?
Honestly: we don't know, and anyone who promises you a number is lying to you. Speed depends on your niche, your output and your consistency. What we control is the system, the feedback and the guarantee — we coach until the work pays.
What happens after I apply?
The application takes about two minutes. If it looks like a fit, you'll book a free 30-minute strategy call where we map your situation and decide together. No pressure tactics, and you already know the price.
